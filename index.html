Features of Letterboxd Tracker Bot

Everything you need to track and share films in your Discord server.

📡

Live Diary Tracking

Automatically posts new Letterboxd diary entries to your chosen channel. Each entry includes the film poster, star rating, liked badge, rewatch indicator, director, genres, runtime, and the written review.

👤

Rich Interactive Profiles

Browse any Letterboxd user's full profile - bio, stats, and favourite films - with their diary, watchlist and lists all in one sortable, paginated, interactive viewer.

🎬

Film Lookup & Compare

Search any film for TMDB metadata - poster, synopsis, cast, director, genres, runtime. Use /compare to see how two users rated the films they've both logged.

📊

Stats & Year Recap

Server diary statistics plus a /recap command for your year in film: total films, average rating, liked count, rewatches, top genres and directors.

🔍

Powerful Filters

Slice a user's logged films by actor, director, genre, decade, or country. Find every Tarantino film someone's watched, or everything they logged from the 90s.

📈

Trending Films

Use /popular to see what films are trending on Letterboxd right now, complete with posters and TMDB details.

🎨

Fully Customisable Embeds

Choose exactly which fields appear in diary embeds - rating, liked badge, rewatch, first-watch label, spoiler reviews, poster, director, genres, runtime, year, watched date. Rename labels and set your own hex accent colour.

🔒

Role-Based Permissions

Assign Discord roles to Admin, Mod, or User command tiers, with fine-grained per-command overrides. Admins always keep full access.

⚡

Smart Adaptive Polling

RSS polling frequency adapts to each user's activity - no webhooks required. Optional quiet hours hold posts overnight and release them once the window ends.

🚀

Interactive Setup Wizard

Run /setup for a step-by-step guided configuration. Set the post channel, add users to track, and configure the bot in just a few minutes.

Quick Setup Guide

Get started in 3 simple steps

1

Add Bot to Your Server

Click the button below to invite the bot to your Discord server. Make sure you have administrator permissions.

Add to Discord →
2

Run Setup Wizard

Type /setup in your server to launch the interactive setup wizard. It guides you through setting the diary post channel and configuring the bot step-by-step.

3

Add Users to Track

Use /adduser @member username to start tracking a Letterboxd user. The bot will automatically post their new diary entries to your designated channel!

Privacy & Data

We collect only what the bot needs to work. Your data is never sold.

What We Store

  • Server settings - channel IDs, embed preferences, timezone
  • Tracked Letterboxd usernames & linked Discord IDs
  • Diary entry IDs (kept 14 days for deduplication)
  • Film history & entry stats for /stats, /compare and /recap
  • Per-user timezone preference
  • Settings audit log (kept 90 days)
  • TMDB film cache - no personal data

What We Never Collect

  • Message content or chat history
  • Passwords, tokens, or credentials
  • Private Letterboxd data - only public diary entries
  • IP addresses or device information
  • Email addresses or payment details

Data Retention

  • 7 days - failed post retry buffer
  • 14 days - seen entry IDs
  • 90 days - settings audit log & TMDB film cache
  • 30 days - film search cache
  • 48 hours - everything is deleted after bot removal

Your Controls

  • /listusers - view all tracked usernames
  • /removeuser - stop tracking someone
  • /settings - manage anonymity & privacy toggles
  • /timezone - set your personal timezone
  • Remove the bot - all data gone within 48 hours
